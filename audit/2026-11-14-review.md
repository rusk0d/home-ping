## 1. Findings

### Medium

| ID | Finding | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| M1 | **"Fridge too cold" is noise: 6 pushes in 5 days.** The fridge's normal low point is just under 0 °C. On 6 of the last 8 days its coldest hour went down to −0.1…−0.4 °C (`hourly.fridge_min`), never lower. Three times it stayed below 0 °C for 30 min: 11-10 09:41, 11-12 01:36 and 11-13 10:34. Each time "Fridge too cold" went out, and "back to normal" 35–50 min later; the 01:36 pair woke the phone at 02:36 local, as quiet hours are off. Most food only starts to freeze somewhere around −1 to −2 °C, so a fridge at −0.3 °C needs no action. | Your call. Either **(a)** `COLD_BELOW` −1.0 °C in `fridge_node.ino`, or **(b)** keep 0 °C and make it last longer (`COLD_FOR` 60–90 min). Both need the backend texts, the app/web gauge and the contracts changed, plus one flash. (a) matches the data better: since the node started (11-06) the fridge has never got below −0.4 °C. |
| M2 | **Every big shopping load ends in an urgent "Fridge too warm".** The probe measures air, and with the door open the air near it warms fast. On 11-12 it went from 1.8 to 13.3 °C in 8 min. "Fridge door open?" came at 16:40, "closed" at 16:53, and then "Fridge too warm" (8 °C for 30 min) at 17:07. That push comes through quiet hours (#351). The fridge was back to normal at 19:27. 11-09 was the same: door at 15:27, "too warm" at 15:50, normal at 16:45. The 30 min starts at the door opening, so after loading it is nearly certain. | Your call. **(a)** A "Loading the fridge" button in the app: the door and warm pushes are held for 1–2 h, then pushed if still on (§4). **(b)** The backend holds `warm_critical` for up to 60 min after a door rise while the temperature is falling, and pushes if it's still above 8 °C after that. **(c)** Leave it: it's a real 12 °C, and the push is right that the fridge is warm. (b) is automatic. (a) is explicit and also covers a door left open on purpose. |

### Low

| ID | Finding | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | **"Fridge door open?" can go out when the fridge is already cooling down.** 11-11: the open push at 18:30:37 (6.4 °C), "closed" at 18:31:36 (6.3 °C). For #411 to close it one reading later, the peak must have been ≥ 6.8 °C. So when it pushed, the fridge was already 0.4 °C below its peak and falling, though the push says "and not cooling down" (`fridge.py:191`). The 10-minute mark only checks that it hasn't fallen 0.5 °C yet. The raw readings of 11-11 are gone (2 days), so this is worked out from the two events. | Push "open" only while the reading is still near the peak, e.g. `temp > peak - 0.2`. Otherwise wait for the next reading, which either closes it or pushes. Replay with `fridge_door_replay.py` first. |
| L2 | **"Fridge door closed" says "Back to 12.8 °C".** Since #411 the door counts as closed once the fridge starts cooling, which can be far above where it started (11-12: 12.8 °C, base 1.8). The body `Back to {temp} °C` (`fridge.py:193`) reads as if it were fine again. | `Cooling down again, 12.8 °C now` when closed by the fall rule; keep `Back to 3.6 °C` for the return to the base. That means `door_step` returns which rule closed it. |
| L3 | **`OCCUPANCY_CHANGE` is 96 % of the events table and means little.** It's logged on every flip of a radar's *raw* occupied flag (`ingest.py:86`). That flips every 10–20 s (median 11-13: occupied 20 s, vacant 10 s), so it's 700–1,200 events per radar per day: 32,818 of 34,140 rows. Everything that reads occupancy smooths it first: 2 of the last 3 rows, a 15 min average. The only reader of the event is `validate_db_deep` check 8, which checks the event itself. | Either log the *smoothed* change (`get_combined_occupancy()` flipping: "the flat became occupied/vacant", a few a day and readable in the history), or drop the event and check 8. Update `db-schema.md`. |
| L4 | **`validate_db_deep` check 11 keeps reporting a bug fixed a week ago.** Its 3 "cooldown violations" are the 10-minute repeats of `warm_low` / `warm_critical_duration` on 11-06/07: the #290 bug fixed in #294. Check 11 reads the whole events table, so they show until they age out on 12-06. A real new violation would look the same. | A `--days` window, default 7, for the event checks. Or skip events from before a cutoff given in the tool. |
| L5 | **Six backend tests fail at certain minutes of the day.** Five of them set quiet hours to `00:00`–`23:59` (`test_issue176`, `206`, `224`, `237`, `305`). That window isn't quiet during 23:59 local, because `alerts.py:173` uses `start <= t < end`. `test_issue331` adds a reading 1 min ago and expects it in the current hour (`:61`), which fails in the first minute of every hour. Seen on 11-09 at 00:00 (5 failed, then 1, then none). CI runs with `TZ=Europe/Budapest`, so a push at 22:59 UTC fails for no reason. | A `support.quiet_now()` helper that sets the window to now ± 1 h, used by all five. `test_issue331`: add the reading at the start of the current hour. |

| R1 | **Fridge limits, now in 4 places** | 0 / 7 / 8 °C live in `fridge_node.ino`, in `fridge.py` (texts, `NORMAL_RANGE`), in the app (`Levels.FRIDGE`, `FridgeCard.status`) and in the web page (`index.html` gauge, `i18n.js`). #406 took 4 PRs for one number, and M1 would take 4 more.
